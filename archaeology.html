THE METHOD

From discovery tointerpretation.

Archaeological knowledge is built through careful observation, recording, comparison and evidence-based interpretation.

01 ⌖

Context

Where an object is found can be as important as the object itself. Its location and relationships provide clues.

02 ▤/b>

Documentation

Plans, photographs, notes and measurements create a record that can be studied long after fieldwork ends.

03 ◇

Analysis

Materials, inscriptions, styles and scientific evidence can be compared to understand an object's history.

04 ✦

Interpretation

Researchers combine evidence to develop explanations while recognising uncertainty and competing possibilities.
