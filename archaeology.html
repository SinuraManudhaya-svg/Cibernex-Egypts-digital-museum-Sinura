THE METHOD

From excavation to museum record.

01

Context

Where an object is found can be as important as the object itself.

02

Documentation

Archaeologists record location, layers, measurements, materials and associated finds.

03

Analysis

Objects may be studied through materials, inscriptions, style, technology and comparison.

04

Interpretation

Researchers combine evidence while acknowledging uncertainty and competing explanations.
