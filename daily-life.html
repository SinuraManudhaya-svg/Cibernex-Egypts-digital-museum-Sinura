A DAY IN EGYPT

History is also ordinary life.

Everyday routines reveal how communities depended on the Nile, specialized work, food systems, households and social relationships.

The agricultural cycle was closely tied to the Nile and the seasons. Farming communities produced grain, vegetables and other resources.

Work & craft

Artisans produced pottery, textiles, tools, jewellery and monuments. Specialized skills were passed through workshops and communities.

Foodways included bread, beer, vegetables, dates, fish and other foods depending on place, period and social status.
