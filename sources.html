Sources &Methodology

A digital museum is an interpretation. This page explains how to approach the information presented throughout the project.

OUR APPROACH

Research should betraceable.

01

Evidence first

Descriptions should be grounded in identifiable objects, historical evidence or reputable research.

02

Context matters

An artifact is interpreted through its period, location, material, use and relationship to other evidence.

03

Uncertainty is real

Ancient history contains unanswered questions. Educational summaries should distinguish evidence from interpretation.
